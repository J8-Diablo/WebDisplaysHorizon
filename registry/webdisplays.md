## Description

```markdown
**WebDisplays** puts working web browsers on screens in your world: build a wall of screen
blocks, click it, type an address. Videos, live streams, any site.

This is the mod's content: its blocks, items, recipes and translations (English and French).
**By itself it does nothing.** The behaviour is in WebDisplays Native.

## It is one of three

WebDisplays for Horizon comes in three parts, and it needs all of them:

- **WebDisplays** (`webdisplays`): this one, the blocks and items.
- **WebDisplays Native** (`webdisplays_native`): the code.
- **HCEF** (`hcef`): the browser.

Install the three, build, then download the browser once from **Options > Mods > HCEF** in
the game. The **WebDisplays Pack** modpack installs the three together.

## What is in it

- Screen blocks, and a stylus to trace screens of any outline.
- Keyboard, remote control, redstone controller, linking tool, configurator, laser pointer,
  MinePad.
- Three speakers: tweeter, bass and full range.
- The crafting components and recipes, and six advancements.

## Good to know

- Windows x64 only for now, and an early version.
- A world opened without this mod loses its WebDisplays blocks: keep it turned on.
- A rewrite for Horizon of the WebDisplays mod by montoyo and the CinemaMod Group.
```
